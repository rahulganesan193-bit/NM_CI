# Comprehensive Empathy Map Canvas: Core Incident Stakeholder Personas
## 1. Executive Summary & Persona Mapping Methodology
The empathy maps focus on people who create, triage and manage Incident records.

## 2. Persona 1: Service Desk Analyst
### 2.1. Persona Context & Behavioral Profile
The analyst needs complete, consistent and correctly routed Incident data.
### 2.2. Service Desk Analyst's Empathy Map Canvas
| Says | Thinks | Does | Feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| “I need complete information.” | “Is this routed correctly?” | Reviews Incident fields | Frustrated by missing data |

## 3. Persona 2: Incident Requester / User
### 3.1. Persona Context & Behavioral Profile
The user wants a simple form with immediate guidance.
### 3.2. Requester's Empathy Map Canvas
| Says | Thinks | Does | Feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| “Tell me what is required.” | “Why is this field blocked?” | Enters and updates fields | Confident when guidance is immediate |

## 4. Persona 3: Service Manager / Assignment Lead
### 4.1. Persona Context & Behavioral Profile
The manager depends on reliable priority, impact and assignment information.
### 4.2. Manager's Empathy Map Canvas
| Says | Thinks | Does | Feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| “High-impact incidents need the right group.” | “Can I trust the record?” | Reviews queues and reports | Concerned about inconsistent data |

## 5. Cross-Persona Comparative Synthesis & Intersecting Failure Points
### 5.1. The Vicious Cycle of Manual Validation
Missing rules create incomplete records, which create additional review effort and delayed routing.

## 6. Architectural Translation: How UI Policies and Client Scripts Deliver Persona Gains
### 6.1. Architectural Realization for the Service Desk Analyst
Mandatory Assignment group and automatic Urgency improve triage quality.
### 6.2. Architectural Realization for the Requester
Warnings and dynamic field behavior provide immediate guidance.
### 6.3. Architectural Realization for the Service Manager
More consistent Incident records improve operational oversight.

## 7. Conclusion & Design Sign-Off
The selected design addresses the common needs of all three personas without replacing the standard Incident interface.
